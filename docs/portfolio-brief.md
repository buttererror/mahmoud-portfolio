This document owns the product and public-content direction for Mahmoud Ahmed's portfolio. It is a working brief, not a locked specification. Raise meaningful tradeoffs when new evidence suggests a better direction.

## Project Goal

Build a professional, recruiter-focused portfolio for a Full-Stack Developer with 7+ years of frontend experience and current hands-on work across interfaces, APIs, relational data, authentication, validation, and automated testing.

The portfolio should support job applications, LinkedIn, GitHub, and resume refinement. It should demonstrate credible engineering work through clear evidence rather than heavy animation or inflated claims.

## Public Safety

The portfolio must be public-safe.

Do not expose:

- Private company code, internal workflows, or confidential architecture.
- Private dashboard screenshots, credentials, APIs, domains, or implementation details.
- Unsupported metrics, responsibilities, project facts, or technologies.
- Email address or phone number unless Mahmoud explicitly changes the contact decision.
- Sensitive workflows or private implementation details from the independent healthcare application.

It is acceptable to use approved company names, broad industries, generalized product descriptions, verified technologies, and approved public links.
